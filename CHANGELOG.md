# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19
First release, for Bedrock and Java (Fabric 26.3):
- Four rockets:
  - a water bottle rocket you fill and pump up by hand;
  - a model rocket with a gunpowder motor and a parachute;
  - a one-seat sky rocket that flies about 100 blocks up;
  - a space rocket for four that flies to the build limit.
- Riders come down under a steerable parachute and never take fall damage. The rockets parachute down too and can fly again.
- Launch pads, and Mission Control: a 10-second countdown on everyone's screen that launches every fuelled rocket on a pad nearby.
- A jetpack (worn in the chest slot, refuelled with Rocket Fuel, catches you when you fall fast) and rocket boosts.
- Rocket Manual, recipes, English and Danish.
